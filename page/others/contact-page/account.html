Account FAQ

Where do I signup?

Signing up for an account on TMDB is free and can be done here. Only a valid email is needed.

What are the benefits?

Registered users can edit movie data, rate content, manage watchlists, create custom lists, and participate in discussions.

Can I import an IMDb list?

Yes. You can import CSV files from IMDb to sync with your TMDB lists, such as watchlists or favorites.

Can I have my own avatar?

Yes. TMDB supports Gravatar or custom image uploads.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, you can delete your account from the account settings page.

Can I share my lists?

Yes, your watchlist, favorite, and rated lists can be set to public or private.

Do rated movies leave the watchlist?

Yes, by default. You can change this setting in your watchlist preferences.

How do you build recommendations?

Recommendations are built based on your ratings. Adding at least 20 ratings provides better results.
